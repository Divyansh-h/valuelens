# Simulated Win-Back Campaign Design: "Operation Hidden Gem"

Based on the findings from our CLV and RFM modeling, we have identified a critical segment of customers who represent massive future value but are currently exhibiting high flight risk. To prevent churn and recapture this revenue, we have designed the following simulated win-back campaign.

## 1. Target Audience
- **The "Hidden Gems" Segment**: 720 specific customers.
- **Selection Criteria**: 
  - Strictly classified as `At Risk (High Value)` or `Lost` under traditional RFM heuristics.
  - Mathematically ranked in the Top Quartile (Q4) for expected 12-Month Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
- **Revenue at Stake**: ~£3.66 Million over the next 12 months.

## 2. The Intervention Strategy
Since these customers are historically high spenders who have abruptly gone quiet, a generic "10% off" coupon is unlikely to break through their inbox noise. We need a premium, personalized offer.

- **Campaign Name**: VIP Re-Engagement Initiative
- **Channel**: Personalized Email + SMS Follow-up (if opted-in)
- **The Offer**: "We've missed you! Here is a £50 VIP credit towards your next purchase of £200 or more."
- **Psychological Hook**: Framing the discount as a "VIP credit" acknowledges their past high-value status with the brand, while the £200 minimum threshold protects profit margins while encouraging their historically large basket sizes.

## 3. Experimental Setup (A/B Testing)
To definitively prove the ROI of this campaign, we will run a randomized controlled trial:
- **Treatment Group (80%)**: 576 customers will receive the VIP Re-Engagement Offer.
- **Control Group (20%)**: 144 customers will be held out and receive no intervention (or standard marketing newsletter).

## 4. Measurable Outcomes & Success Metrics
The campaign's success will be tracked over a strict 60-day observation window following the intervention.

### Primary Metric:
* **60-Day Reactivation Rate**: The percentage of customers in the Treatment group who complete a purchase within 60 days, compared to the baseline organic reactivation rate of the Control group.

### Secondary Metrics:
* **Incremental Revenue Lift**: The total gross revenue generated by the Treatment group minus the expected gross revenue (based on the Control group's behavior). 
* **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Total incremental revenue divided by the total cost of the redeemed £50 credits.
* **Average Basket Size (AOV)**: Did the £200 threshold successfully drive larger cart sizes upon reactivation?

## 5. Next Steps
If the simulation or live execution of this campaign demonstrates a statistically significant lift in reactivation and positive ROAS, this intervention logic should be automated. The CLV pipeline can trigger an API call to our email marketing platform whenever a Q4-CLV customer slips into the "At Risk" RFM segment.
